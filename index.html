Frequently Asked Questions

What is the return policy?
You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. To start a return, contact our customer support team with your order number. Refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.
When will I receive my refund?
Refunds are typically processed within 5–10 business days after we receive and inspect your returned item. Once the refund has been issued, it may take a few additional days for your bank or card provider to post the funds to your account.
Where are you located?
We are located in the heart of the city, just a short walk from the main square. Our store is open Monday to Saturday, 9am–6pm. You can also reach us online anytime through our contact page.
